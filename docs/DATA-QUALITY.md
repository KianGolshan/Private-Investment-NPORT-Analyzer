# Data Quality Rules

Every rule comes from a real failure in SEC data. One line per trap here; the real examples, counts and the test
that pins each are in [archive/DATA-QUALITY-full.md](archive/DATA-QUALITY-full.md). Accessions are in
[GOLDEN-NUMBERS.md](GOLDEN-NUMBERS.md).

## Reporting cadence (read first)

- One NPORT-P per fund per **fiscal** quarter; months 1–2 are not public. Calendars are staggered (Capital Group
  Feb/May/Aug/Nov and Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec, many Fidelity funds Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct). Filings arrive ~60 days after the
  report date; bulk data ends at the last quarter-end, so the newest marks are in the catch-up.
- **Never bucket by calendar quarter.** Use as-of semantics (ADR 0004) and show every fund's mark date.

## Display rules

- Every value shows its fund's **mark date** and links its accession. "As of D" mixes dates.
- Changes are worded as filings allow: "first reported", "added", "reduced", "no longer reported", "reported at $0",
  "mark moved". Never a guessed cause (sale, tender, write-off).
- A per-share mark comes from share rows only; units, contracts and principal are per unit (trap 40). Funds are
  compared as filed at the same mark date (LESSONS 39).
- "Indirect" = held through a named SPV (`via_spv`) **or** a row the filer files as a fund interest (instrument
  type indirect, e.g. Coatue's "private fund" rows); one definition, `asof.kindOf` (direct | spv | fund), used by
  holders, the market list and filters. Opaque-SPV exposure shows its basis in the filing's words.
- A change splits into a **position effect** (shares added or removed, at the prior mark; a new or dropped class at
  its value) and a **mark effect** (the new mark on the shares held), which sum to the value change exactly; a split
  is neither (F13), a row with no share count is "value only", and "reported at $0" is a mark effect
  (`activity.leg`; F39).
- **Bridge** (`analysis.bridge`, P6b W1): start value + first reported + added − reduced − no longer reported ± mark
  moved ± value only + started filing − stopped filing = end value, to the cent; start and end are the as-of rule.
  "Started filing" is a holding at a fund's first stored filing or after a gap over 123 days; "stopped filing" is a
  fund dropping out for want of a filing. Position facts (migration 0019) hold these legs for private companies.
- **Scope:** firm, fund, class and kind filters narrow the rows every answer is built on (`services/scope.js`), so a
  filtered answer equals a post-filter of the unfiltered one; a class includes its segregated lines (trap 52).
- **Mark leadership** (`marks.markLeadership`): a firm "first filed" a per-share level when its own mark moved
  over 0.5% to it before any other firm's did (levels within 0.5%); every adopter shows its previous mark date,
  because staggered calendars decide who can file first. It compares firms, so it always reads every firm. Never
  "led" or "followed" as a cause (F49).
- **Marks vs others** (`analysis.marksVsOthers`, firm and fund pages): a firm's (or fund's) median per-share mark
  per class against the median of every other fund that filed the same class at the **same** mark date; no other
  fund that date = no comparison, shown as such (staggered calendars leave most Fidelity classes uncompared: 123 of
  460). "Above", "same" (within 0.5%), "below" as filed (F50).
- **Timeline:** a bar while any of the firm's funds is in force with the company (the as-of rule); a holding in a
  fund's first stored filing reads "held at the first stored filing", never "first reported" (the data starts 2019).
- **Explore drill** (`analysis.drill`, P6b W4): a cell's legs are the ones the pivot summed: a level lists the legs
  in force at the period end, a flow the legs whose change the bridge puts in that step, at their own mark date (a
  fund that stopped filing at the day it dropped out). They sum to the cell, grouped by filing.
- **Movers** (`analysis.movers`): each company's bridge over the window, ranked by mark effect and by net position
  flow (first reported + added − reduced − no longer reported). Worded as filed; a share exchange that is not a
  split reads as added + mark moved (Mesquite, F52).
- **Newly reported** (`analysis.newlyReported`): the company's first stored holding with value, any fund, falls in
  the window; the first stored quarter (2019Q4) never counts as new.
- **Firms are the current adviser** (latest N-CEN). Dated attribution was measured and not adopted: real firm-to-firm
  adviser changes touch 16 private-company filings, $0.05B of $640.6B filed (0.01%; $0.28B with retired adviser
  entities not in `managers.csv`); answers say "current adviser (latest N-CEN)".
- **Periods:** levels are as of the period end; changes are dated by each fund's own mark date and summed over the
  window ("changes in filings with mark dates in …"); a mark date is never relabeled to a quarter end.
- The quarter still being filed is partial.

## Open questions

- Fidelity's opaque per-fund vehicles (~$0.6B: VETERINARY, AB, TC, TB, TRB, TB2, THRIVE HOLDINGS LLCs): no filing names
  their targets; they stay on the review list.
- `companies.public_since` is empty; listing evidence covers only the latest 4 bulk quarters (P8 research task).
