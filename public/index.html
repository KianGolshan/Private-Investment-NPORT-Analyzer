About Vantage

What this is

Vantage pulls valuation data directly from SEC EDGAR filings — NPORT-P holdings reports and BDC 10-Q schedules of investments — to show how institutional funds are exposed to a given private company and how they mark that position over time.

Who it's for

Analysts and investors who need visibility into private-company valuations across multiple funds without opening each filing by hand. Useful for spot-checking a fund's mark against its peers, tracking a valuation trend across quarters, or building a data set for further analysis.

How it works

Search by company name or ticker. Vantage queries EDGAR's full-text search, parses the matching filings, and extracts price per share (for equity and fund holdings) or fair-value mark as a percentage of par (for private credit and BDC positions). Results can be filtered by date, filtered by class, charted, and exported to CSV, Excel, or PDF.

Data source

All data comes directly from SEC EDGAR. This tool does not modify, estimate, or supplement filed figures — it extracts and organizes what funds have already disclosed.

Pulls a fund's own NPORT-P filing in full (not filtered to one security) and identifies its private EQUITY interests — common/preferred stock, warrants, and indirect/SPV vehicles in privately-held companies — using the filing's own SEC fair-value hierarchy: Level 3 only (valued with unobservable inputs, i.e. no real market for it). Bonds/loans are excluded even if Level 3, and Level 2 "restricted" securities (e.g. foreign-ownership-restricted but still publicly-traded stock) are excluded too, since neither is a privately-held equity stake.
