SEC Private Investment Valuation Reporting

Analyze real institutional holdings from NPORT-P filings

Why this exists

Institutional investors comparing how different funds mark the same private holding traditionally do it by hand — opening dozens of SEC filings one at a time and copying numbers into a spreadsheet, easily tens of hours across a real coverage list. This tool queries EDGAR directly, extracts the price/mark from every matching filing, and lets you chart peer marks, benchmark them against a reference price, and export the result — in seconds.

Real SEC Data Parses actual NPORT-P filings from the SEC EDGAR database. Designed to compare marks for private investments across funds that publish NPORT filings.
